# Sprint 4 — Your actions

These actions are separate from implementation and can be completed after the build is ready.

1. Open one real job posting in a normal HTTPS Chrome tab.
2. Open Job Copilot and click **Read current job page**.
3. Confirm or correct the extracted job title, company, and description, then save it locally.
4. Click **Analyze on device** and confirm that the recommendation makes sense for that job.

Do not enter application answers or submit an application during this sprint test.
